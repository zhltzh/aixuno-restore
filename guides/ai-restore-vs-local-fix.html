AI Restore vs Local Fix: Which Should You Use?

Aixuno Restore offers two engines on purpose: an instant local fix and a heavier AI Enhance. Picking the right one saves time and protects privacy.

Speed

The local fix is nearly instant, even for large photos. AI Enhance typically takes 10–60 seconds because the models run on CPU.

Privacy

Local processing never uploads anything — ideal for sensitive family photos. AI Enhance uploads once and deletes both upload and result within 24 hours.

What each is good at

Local: yellowing, fading, low contrast, light grain, mild softness. AI: facial detail reconstruction, 2×–4× upscaling, deeper damage.

A practical rule

Try the local fix first and look at the before/after slider. If faces still lack detail or the print is too small to use, run AI Enhance on that photo.
