Fix Scratches, Dust and Grain in Old Photos

Scratches and dust show up as thin bright or dark lines, while grain looks like uniform speckle. They need slightly different handling.

How the local tool helps

The scratch & noise slider applies a 3×3 median filter: every pixel is replaced by the median of its neighborhood, which removes isolated specks without blurring real edges as much as a blur would. Strength is blended with the original so detail is preserved.

Use it sparingly

Start at a low value and inspect the before/after slider. Too much median filtering gives skin a plastic look and can erase fine detail like hair and fabric texture.

When to choose AI instead

Deep scratches that cross faces, large stained areas, or cracks need content-aware reconstruction. AI Enhance with GFPGAN rebuilds facial features that simple filters cannot recover.

Prevention for the next scan

Wipe prints with a clean, dry, soft cloth before scanning and clean the scanner glass. Removing dust physically is always better than removing it digitally.
