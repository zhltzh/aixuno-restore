How to Restore Faded Family Photos Online

Old family prints usually fail in three ways: the paper yellows, contrast washes out, and grain creeps in. The good news is that all three respond well to automated correction.

1. Get a good digital copy

Scan at 300 dpi or higher if you can. No scanner? A phone photo taken in bright, even, indirect light works well — avoid flash and shadows.

2. Start with the local fix

Upload the photo to Aixuno Restore. Fade correction uses gray-world white balance to remove the yellow cast, and auto levels stretch the histogram back to full contrast. For most prints this alone is a dramatic improvement.

3. Add sharpening carefully

Keep sharpening moderate. Too much creates halos around faces and makes grain more visible. If the scan is noisy, turn on scratch & noise removal first, then sharpen.

4. Use AI for faces and small prints

When faces look soft or the original is small, click AI Enhance. GFPGAN rebuilds facial detail and Real-ESRGAN upscales 2×–4× so the photo can be printed again.

5. Save and archive

Download the PNG, keep the original scan untouched, and store both. Future tools will only get better.
