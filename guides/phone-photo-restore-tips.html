Phone Photo Tips for Better Restoration

The quality of your digital copy limits everything that follows. A few small habits make a big difference.

Use even, indirect light

Daylight near a window is ideal. Avoid direct sun, flash and mixed lighting, which cause glare and color casts that are hard to correct.

Shoot straight down

Hold the phone parallel to the print. Tilted shots stretch the image and reduce effective resolution on one side.

Fill the frame, but do not crop into the photo

Leave a small border around the print. You can crop later; you cannot recover detail that was never captured.

Keep the print flat

Place it on a flat surface and smooth curled corners gently with clean, dry hands or a heavy book for a few minutes.

Then restore

Upload to Aixuno Restore, start with the local fix, and use AI Enhance only when faces or small details still look soft.
