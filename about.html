About Aixuno Restore

Aixuno Restore helps you bring old photos back to life. Faded prints, yellowed scans, light scratches and grain can all be improved in seconds, right in your browser.

Local first, AI when you need it

The instant fix uses Canvas processing that never leaves your device. When a photo needs more help, AI Enhance runs open-source models — GFPGAN for face enhancement and Real-ESRGAN for 2×–4× upscaling — on our server, with uploads and results deleted within 24 hours.

Part of the Aixuno family

Aixuno builds free, privacy-friendly browser tools: Markdown conversion, image tools, file compression, PDF utilities, background removal, document scanning, photo restoration and video watermark removal.
